Simply Works Marketing (SWM) helps local restaurants and cafés work with local food and lifestyle creators. We invite creators for a hosted visit and coordinate the details, such as the date, time and party size, on behalf of the restaurant.

SWM Creator Ops is our internal tool for that work. It sends invitations and handles scheduling replies over Instagram and email from SWM's own accounts.
