SWM Creator Ops · Last updated September 27, 2026

SWM Creator Ops is an internal tool run by Simply Works Marketing ("SWM") for inviting creators to partner restaurants and scheduling their visits. It is not offered to the public and has no user sign-up.

Invitations

An invitation describes a hosted visit offered by a partner restaurant. Accepting it is voluntary. The details stated in the invitation and the booking confirmation apply, including the date, party size, what the restaurant covers, and any content that was agreed. Either side may cancel before the visit by replying to the conversation.

Communications

We contact creators by Instagram message and, where a business email address is publicly listed, by email. Reply "stop" to any email to stop further emails.
